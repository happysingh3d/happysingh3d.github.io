42 game assets.
One survival world.

Vehicles, a maintenance yard, props and tools for Project Frontier, my first-person survival game in Unity. Each one is rendered in Blender (Cycles) from three angles. Click any picture to look closer.

AI-assisted game assets in production, not final art: generated in Tripo, then curated, textured and set up for Unity. Rendered in Blender (Cycles) from three angles. Triangle counts are the full-detail mesh; lighter versions are made for the game.
