Getting around

  • Look around by dragging. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Open a building by clicking it or its name tag. Games and websites open full screen; move the mouse to the top edge to bring back the "Back to the park" bar.
  • Walk as the fox with W A S D or the arrow keys. Hold Shift to run.
  • Fly with the Fly button or F. Space goes up, Ctrl (or C) goes down, Shift flies faster. While flying, the fox heads where the camera looks: look down to dive, up to climb. Land to walk again.
  • Look around while exploring by dragging with either mouse button.
  • Day and night change every few minutes. The Night button switches straight away.
  • On a phone, use the stick to walk and the arrows to fly up and down.
