Your everyday

Toolkit brings useful material and texture tasks together in a browser. Make a normal map, preview a material, pack texture channels, or assemble an image atlas.

Open in a full tab ↗
01 / MATERIALS

Normal maps & previews

Generate normal and height maps from an image, then inspect materials in a browser-based viewport.

02 / TEXTURES

Channel packing & atlases

Pack channels and arrange source images into a texture atlas. Export the atlas image and its UV layout data.

03 / WORKFLOW

Maya & Blender helpers

Access the suite’s script generators for common tasks. Review generated scripts before running them in your scene.

The current atlas tool packs images; it does not merge 3D meshes or automatically bake all material maps into one asset. For those workflows, use a suitable DCC tool or plugin.

Toolkit runs on its existing website. Its own controls, browser support, and export behavior apply.
